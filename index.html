How We Work

A simple loop, repeated until the numbers move.

01

Discovery Call

We talk goals, audience and format. I audit what you have and where the drop-offs are.

02

Strategy & Script

Hooks, structure and a content plan. You approve the direction before a single cut happens.

03

Edit & Review

First cut within the agreed turnaround, then two rounds of timestamped revisions.

04

Publish & Iterate

Exports, thumbnails and captions delivered. We read the numbers and adjust the next batch.

Hey, I'm Naitik.

I'm a freelance video editor and content strategist. I spend most of my week in a timeline, figuring out why people stop watching at second nine and making sure they don't.

My work sits between editing and strategy. A good cut is not enough if the hook is weak or the thumbnail promises nothing, so I tend to get involved earlier than an editor usually does.

I work with creators and small brands who want their feed to look like one thing, not forty. Clear systems, consistent output, numbers we can actually read.
